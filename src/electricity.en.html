How to choose an adapter

  • An adapter only changes the plug shape. It does not convert voltage.
  • Israel runs on 230 V. Most of Europe is the same, while the US, Canada, Mexico and Japan use much lower voltage.
  • Phone, laptop and camera chargers are almost always labelled 100–240 V, so they work anywhere with an adapter. Hair dryers, irons and heaters are often voltage-specific, so check them.
  • A small universal adapter saves trouble when you visit more than one country.

Plug and voltage information is based on public lists (Wikipedia and the IEC) and may change. Many countries have several plug types in use at once, especially in older hotels.
